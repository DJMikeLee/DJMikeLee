# Sukwon (Mike) Lee

I’m a computer engineering senior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I study rover localization under soybean canopies by collecting field recordings and comparing them with sensor data from NVIDIA Isaac Sim.

## Robotics research

- **Soybean-field digital twin.** I built a soybean field in Isaac Sim and compared its sensor recordings with public TerraSentia field data. The turning rates and stereo depth estimates differed even when the standardized distributions looked similar. Professor Elhan Ersoz and I coauthored the [research poster](https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/140406). [Comparison notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1Lqp2u4U2sGi2FowX7rdRAzntwRARgSVo)
- **DeepFusion VIO.** I trained a PyTorch model to estimate motion from optical flow and IMU measurements, then compared its trajectories with a camera-only baseline on three EuRoC Machine Hall sequences. [Research poster](https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/137523) · [Colab notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1LZXC65DlAbaRXymiY6DZpzfylQbDGhsk)
